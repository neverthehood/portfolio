About

I'm Vlad Hohlov, Creative Lead and Design Strategist with a track record across high-velocity startups and studio environments.

My work lives at the overlap of creative direction and technical execution: hands-on in 3D, motion, and visual identity, while staying close to the strategic and operational side. Increasingly, that includes integrating AI tools into the workflow. Not as a shortcut, but as a way to move faster without losing creative control.

Lately I've been drawn to packaging, where form, material, and brand all have to work together at once. I also have a strong foundation in vector graphics, which finds its way into most of what I do.

I hold a technical degree, which shaped the way I approach problems: methodically, with an eye on the outcome rather than just the process. I'm goal-oriented by nature. I take ownership of what I start and see it through. That same pragmatism carries into how I work with people: I adapt quickly to different team dynamics, communicate well across disciplines, and enjoy the crossover moments where design, business, and other functions meet. Creative work energizes me, and so does the collaboration that makes it land.
